The idea

Everything you do maps to a plank in the Iowa First platform. Plant cover crops and prairie strips beside brown water and it runs clear. Nitrate slimes crawl out of dirty water at night; clean the water and they stop coming. Eat Iowa-grown food to keep your Real Food meter up; the vending machine stuff spikes and crashes.

Find the yellow Taylorcraft on the northeast lawn by the road, look at it and press E to fly. Press E again to set down.
