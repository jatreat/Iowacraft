# Iowacraft

A fan-made, Minecraft-style voxel game about the Iowa First campaign. Play it here: **https://jatreat.github.io/iowacraft/**

Start on the family farm outside Belle Plaine, plant cover crops until the creek runs clear, take samples to the lab, take on the Big Ag cartel, put Iowa-grown food in the school cafeteria, fly the Taylorcraft, and make it to Terrace Hill by Election Night.

- Single file (`index.html`), no build step, no server. Three.js r128 from cdnjs; every texture is painted in the browser at startup.
- Works on desktop (mouse + WASD) and phones (touch controls).
- Autosaves in the browser.

Fan-made by a supporter. Not authorized by, paid for by, or affiliated with Lahn for Governor, MAHA Action, or any committee. Every quoted line is from Zach Lahn's website, debates, interviews, or news coverage; the Sources tab in the in-game Campaign Notebook lists them. Neighbors Ryan and Ashley Wallace appear with invented dialogue; all other characters are fictional.
